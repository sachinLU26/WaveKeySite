## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, high-assurance cybersecurity experience tailored for C-suite security officers, devops architects, and enterprise infrastructure teams. Built around continuous ultrasonic proximity MFA, the brand communicates ambient vigilance, cryptographic certainty, and seamless frictionless authentication.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision High-Contrast Dark Mode** with **Refined Technical Glassmorphism**. Deep obsidian and void surfaces create an infinite operational canvas, accented by crystalline acoustic cyan waves indicating verified presence and sharp crimson alerts highlighting intercepted threat vectors. Layouts prioritize technical clarity, rapid scannability, mathematical density, and tactile data telemetry.

## Colors

The palette leverages pure optical contrast between profound dark backgrounds and energized signal channels:

- **Primary Cyan (`#00F2FE`) & Acoustic Teal (`#00D2B4`):** Represents legitimate telemetry, verified proximity, active ultrasonic handshakes, and operational security integrity.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Blue (`#4FACFE`):** Connects active data channels, gradients, and supportive state tags.
- **Obsidian Neutrals (`#080B10`, `#0B0F17`, `#0F1420`):** Provide deep, glare-free optical comfort while allowing illuminated signal states to stand out.
- **Threat Vector Accents (`#FF3366` & `#FF6B4A`):** Exclusively deployed across threat mitigation metrics, adversarial vectors, intercepted session hijacking instances, and alert badges.
- **Hierarchy of Whites (`#F8FAFC`, `#94A3B8`, `#475569`):** Ensures uncompromising readability under demanding SOC and operational conditions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured geometric edge of **Space Grotesk** for prominent headers with the neutral, hyper-legible clarity of **Inter** for descriptions and interactive elements. 

**JetBrains Mono** is leveraged strictly for micro-copy, status markers, ultrasonic frequencies (`18.5-21 kHz`), step counters (`01`, `02`), and threat telemetry. Headings feature tight tracking to convey high density and precision. Always enforce high contrast by binding main headings to `#F8FAFC` and secondary explanatory text to `#94A3B8`.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on an 8pt architectural grid with a fluid 12-column desktop structure (max width `1280px`). 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` section margins. Complex data cards leverage 4-column spans, while hero narrative and audio visualization diagrams utilize 6-column pairings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins, collapsing multi-metric cards into 2-column pairs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` edge margins. Elements reflow into pure single-column stacks with touch-optimized vertical rhythm (`space-lg` separation).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered semi-transparent obsidian surfaces combined with selective luminous backdrops rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#080B10` with subtle background vector noise or concentric ultrasonic wave lines.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Translucent `#0F1420` at 70% opacity backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and bounded by a razor-thin 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Panels & Flyouts):** Solidified `#151C2C` at 90% opacity with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.12)`).
- **Threat State Elevation:** Attack and anomaly modules replace the cool cyan rim lighting with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.4)`) and focused amber-crimson dissipation (`box-shadow: 0 0 30px -8px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a sleek, tactical silhouette with tight corner rounding (`roundedness: 1` — base 4px / `0.25rem`). Cards, inputs, and interactive surfaces favor controlled, angular geometry over playful organic curves, reinforcing architectural defense and cryptographic rigor.

Large containers and structural glass panels expand to a maximum radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), while small status chips and telemetry tags maintain sharp `2px` to `4px` borders. Pill shapes are strictly avoided except for dynamic presence ping indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Signal Active):** Background linear gradient from `#00F2FE` to `#4FACFE`, solid black `#080B10` bold text, `0.25rem` radius, subtle `0 0 16px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.4)` glow on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#0F1420` surface with 1px `border-subtle`, white `#F8FAFC` text, shifting to neon cyan border illumination on focus or hover.
- **Threat Response / Destructive:** Crimson background `#FF3366` with crisp white text, pulsing alert glow on active warning events.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Encased in glassmorphic obsidian (`#0F1420` / 75% opacity) with 1px border.
- Headers integrate a monospaced tracking label (`label-caps`) paired with an authentic status indicator dot (glowing green/cyan `#00D2B4` for continuous verified sessions; pulsing red `#FF3366` for threat vector mitigation).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls use deeply recessed dark fill (`#0B0F17`) framed by 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus states transition the perimeter border to `#00F2FE` with a soft 2px ambient focus ring. Labels sit above inputs in uppercase `label-code` format.

### Acoustic Presence Indicators & Chips
- Status badges feature monospaced JetBrains Mono typography, preceded by an oscillating ping ring animating continuous verification (`19.5-21 kHz Active`).
- Threat chips feature high-contrast crimson backgrounds with `threat-amber` text for instantaneous hazard recognition.
